THE BEAST / 100 M / 200 M

Yohan
Blake

Jamaica’s sprint champion

Yohan Blake became the youngest 100-metre world champion in 2011. At the 2012 Olympics, he won silver in both the 100 m and 200 m, then helped Jamaica set a world record in the 4 × 100 m relay.

How his career grew

  1. 2011 / Breakthrough

    Won the 100 m world title and took silver in the 200 m, becoming the youngest world champion in the 100 m.

  2. 2012 / Olympic success

    Earned silver in both individual sprints at the London Olympics and gold in Jamaica’s world-record 4 × 100 m relay.

  3. Personal bests

    His best times reached 9.69 seconds for 100 m and 19.26 seconds for 200 m, placing him among the fastest sprinters ever.

Training for speed

Blake trained with coach Glen Mills at Racers Track Club in Jamaica. Sprint preparation for the 100 m and 200 m includes work such as:

  • Starts and acceleration

    Practising powerful first steps out of the blocks.

  • Top-speed technique

    Building efficient sprint form and maintaining speed.

  • Speed endurance and strength

    Developing the power to sustain a fast pace through the race.

These are common sprint-training components, not a verified day-by-day list of Blake’s personal workouts.
